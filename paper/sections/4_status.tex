\section{Status, deviations and limitations}
\label{sec:status}

This is a working draft; \cref{tab:status} lists every pre-registered experiment and its verdict at the time of the
build. Changes to the plan made along the way are logged with reasons and approvals; the ones that matter for reading
the results are: the learning-rate gate failed twice before passing, first because unconditional drifting was
mis-specified (repulsion within each condition's samples instead of the whole batch) and then narrowly; a
single-width kernel transported mass too locally and was replaced by a mixture of widths; 64 instead of 8 samples per
condition are generated, since self-only repulsion estimated from 8 samples is strongly biased; and the drift
normalisation was corrected from per-term to whole-field, as in the original method, after the per-term version flung
single-positive samples outside the data. Runs made before a correction are kept and excluded.

The toy uses an oracle condition embedding; text embeddings, learned features and real captions are later stages of
the plan, not reported here.
