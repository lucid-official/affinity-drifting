\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Affinity Drifting builds on drifting models~\cite{deng2026drifting}, which turn kernel
mean-shift~\cite{comaniciu2002meanshift} into a training signal for one-step generators. Its positive distribution is
a Nadaraya--Watson kernel estimate~\cite{nadaraya1964,watson1964} of the conditional distribution, with the bandwidth
set by the effective sample size of the weights~\cite{kish1965survey}. The field is closely related to gradients of
kernel two-sample statistics (MMD)~\cite{gretton2012kernel}, which generative models have combined with mixtures of
kernel scales for the same reason we do~\cite{li2017mmdgan}. The evaluation of mode collapse follows standard
practice: precision and recall~\cite{kynkaanniemi2019improved} and density and coverage~\cite{naeem2020reliable}.
Capacity is controlled by quantizing the condition and by spectral normalization~\cite{miyato2018spectral}.
